AceStudy — Exam Prep SaaS

A MERN-stack study tracker built for Indian competitive-exam aspirants preparing for JEE, NEET, GATE, UPSC and CAT. Includes fully client-side focus-session face detection (face-api.js with TinyFaceDetector), a logistic-regression "crack probability" model, streaks and gamification, JWT authentication, and Zustand for state management.

Currently expanding the feature set with Pomodoro timers, configurable breaks, per-exam subject customization, a daily planner and mock-test logs, benchmarked against category-leading study apps.
