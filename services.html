// Engagement style

Pick how you'd rather work

Project-based Ongoing retainer

Project-based

A defined scope, a fixed price, a fixed timeline. Best when you know what you need built and want cost certainty from day one. Covers everything from a landing page to a full SaaS MVP.

Ongoing retainer

A set number of hours each month for maintenance, small features and priority support. Best once you're live and need a developer on call without re-scoping every change.

// FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?

+

A landing page usually takes 3–5 days; a full business website 1–2 weeks; a SaaS build 4–8 weeks depending on scope, agreed upfront in the proposal.

Do you work with clients outside Ranchi?

+

Yes — most of my clients are remote. I'm available for in-person meetings if you're in Ranchi, and calls/WhatsApp for everyone else.

What do you need from me to get started?

+

A short brief on your goals and audience, any brand assets you already have, and 30 minutes for a discovery call — I'll guide the rest.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

+

Yes, monthly maintenance packages are available covering updates, monitoring and small fixes — details shared after launch.

Who owns the code once the project's done?

+

You do, fully. Full source and asset handoff at launch — no dependency on me to make future changes if you choose not to continue working together.

How do payments work?

+

A deposit to begin work after scope sign-off, with the balance due at launch — or split into milestones for larger SaaS builds. Details are always in the written proposal.
