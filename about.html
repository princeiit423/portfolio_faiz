I'm Faiz Hussain, a full-stack web developer based in Ranchi, Jharkhand, currently completing my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at Asansol Engineering College, affiliated with MAKAUT. I've been building and shipping digital products for clients across India since 2022.

My background in electronics gave me a habit that carried straight into web development: understand the system before you touch the interface. That shows up in how I work — I plan the data model and the user flow before I write a line of CSS, which is part of why client sites end up fast, structured, and easy to extend later.

Over the last few years I've moved from single-page business sites to full SaaS builds — a MERN-stack exam-prep platform with client-side face detection, and a destination-wedding marketplace with a multi-country venue directory. Each project pushed me to get better at the parts that don't show up in a screenshot: authentication, data modelling, performance, and SEO.

"If the architecture is right, the interface almost designs itself."

// How I Work

Principles, not just code.

Plan before you build

Data model and user flow come before any UI — it's why client sites stay easy to extend months later.

Plain-language updates

No jargon walls — you always know what's done, what's next, and what a tradeoff actually costs you.

Built to convert

Every layout decision is judged against one question: does this make it easier to say yes?

You own the outcome

Full code and asset handoff at launch — no dependency on me to make changes down the line.

Off the clock

Most of what I learn doesn't come from client work — it comes from weekend hardware builds and hackathon sprints, where there's no scope document and the only deadline is the judging round. That's usually where a new tool earns a permanent place in the stack before it ever reaches a client project.
